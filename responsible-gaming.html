Responsible Gaming Policy

Guidelines for safe, disciplined, and healthy online entertainment.

Participation in real-money gaming is legally restricted to individuals aged 18 years or older. 💡 3. Best Practices for Responsible Play

  • ✔
    Set a Fixed Budget: Decide on a strict daily or weekly bankroll before you play. Never exceed this amount, regardless of wins or losses.
  • ✔
    Avoid Chasing Losses: Trying to recover lost money urgently often leads to bigger mistakes. Accept losses as entertainment expense and stop playing.
  • ✔
    Track Your Screen Time: Take regular breaks. Playing continuously impairs judgment, analytical calculations, and decision-making.
  • ✔
    Never Borrow Funds: Do not use credit cards, loans, or household funds for mobile gaming apps. Only play with surplus pocket money.

🚩 4. Signs of Compulsive Gaming

Ask yourself these self-assessment questions to determine if your gaming habits need adjustment:

  • • Do you spend more time or money on gaming apps than initially intended?
  • • Do you feel restless or irritable when trying to cut down on game sessions?
  • • Have you neglected work, family duties, or sleep to continue playing?
  • • Do you play as an emotional escape from stress, anxiety, or loneliness?

🛡️ 5. Self-Exclusion & Assistance

If you feel gaming is affecting your personal or financial well-being, we strongly urge you to use the self-exclusion features provided inside specific game apps (such as temporary account freezing or deposit caps). You can also seek confidential guidance from professional mental health counselors.
